Our story

A corner café built around the roaster.

Ember & Bean started with a tiny drum roaster and a simple idea: coffee tastes best when it's roasted just steps from your cup.

Every week we roast small batches right in the shop, bake from scratch each morning, and keep a table open for regulars and newcomers alike.
